Look closer

The parts nobody
photographs.

  • Flatlock seam

    Sits flat against skin instead of ridging up. It is the seam that decides whether a training tee is worn twice or worn out.

  • Ribbed cuff

    Knitted with enough recovery to still grip the wrist after thirty washes, not just on the day it ships.

  • Bar tack

    Pocket mouths and drawcord exits get reinforced where the stress actually lands. Cheap runs skip this and split there first.

  • Woven label

    Your mark, sewn into the neck seam. The one part of the garment that says who it belongs to.

From enquiry to dispatch

How an order actually runs

Step 01

The spec

You send style, quantity, colours and any branding. We come back with fabric options, pricing and a realistic lead time, usually inside one business day.

Step 02

Sampling

We cut a sample against your size set so fit, fabric weight and print placement are settled on cloth rather than on a screen.

Step 03

Cutting

Once the sample is approved, the full lay is marked and cut. Panels are bundled per size so nothing drifts during assembly.

Step 04

Production

Stitching, branding and finishing run back to back, so a delay at one station gets caught the same day instead of surfacing at delivery.

Step 05

Dispatch

Checked, sorted by size, poly-packed and invoiced under GST, then handed to transport for delivery anywhere in India.

About D. Sant

Small enough to answer, set up to run bulk

Started in 2024 out of Rohini, Delhi, D. Sant is a wholesale-only manufacturer. We supply distributors, multi-brand retailers, private-label owners, corporate uniform buyers and teams. We do not sell single pieces to the public.

We keep the team small on purpose. It means shorter approval chains, direct contact with the person cutting your fabric, and room to take both bulk wholesale orders and smaller custom runs.
