Privacy policy

How we handle the information you send through the trade enquiry form. Last updated 24 August 2026.

This policy is provided in good faith and is not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a qualified professional before relying on it.

Who is responsible

D. Sant (“we”), a proprietorship registered under GST 07AIWPG6817F1ZX, decides how and why the data described below is processed. For any question about your data, or to make a request under the rights listed further down, contact:

  • Grievance officer: D. Sant, Head of Operations
  • Email: sales.dsant@gmail.com
  • Phone: +91 72920 02551
  • Address: 1450, Ground Floor Hall 1, Pooth Kalan, Kanjhawala Road, Rohini, Delhi, India – 110086

India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 requires a named contact for data grievances. We aim to respond to any request within 30 days.

What we collect

Only what the enquiry form asks for, and only when you choose to submit it:

  • Contact details — your name, company, email address and phone number.
  • Business details — buyer type and, if you choose to provide it, your GSTIN.
  • Enquiry details — the product, quantity, branding requirement and any message.
  • Country of request — derived from the connection, used to spot abuse.

We do not store your IP address, we do not fingerprint your device, and we do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The only thing kept in your browser is your light or dark theme preference, which never leaves your device.

Why we use it

To read your enquiry, prepare a quote and reply to you. That is the whole purpose. We do not sell, rent or share your details with anyone for marketing, and we will not add you to a mailing list because you asked for a quote.

Who else sees it

Three processors handle your data on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare — hosts the site and the database that stores enquiries.
  • Resend — delivers the confirmation email to you and the notification to us.
  • Telegram — if enabled, receives an alert that an enquiry arrived.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are deleted after 24 months. If a quote leads to an order, the related records may be kept longer where tax or accounting law requires it.

How it is protected

  • The whole site is served over HTTPS, with HSTS and a content security policy.
  • The database is encrypted at rest by the hosting provider.
  • Submissions are rate limited and validated on the server.
  • Credentials are held in an encrypted secret store, never in the site’s code.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise. If a breach affects your data we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or explain how it was used. Email the address above and we will act on it. If you are unhappy with our response you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.
