Terms of supply

How we quote, produce and deliver wholesale orders. Last updated 25 August 2026.

These terms apply to wholesale supply between businesses. They are provided in good faith and are not legal advice — have them reviewed by a qualified professional before relying on them. Where a signed purchase order or supply agreement says something different, that document takes precedence.

1. Quotations

Quotations are valid for 15 days from the date issued unless stated otherwise. Prices depend on fabric rates, which move; beyond that window a quotation may be revised. A quotation is an invitation to order, not a confirmed booking of capacity.

2. Minimum order

Minimum order is 100 pieces per style, in full size sets. Some styles carry a higher minimum; the quotation states it. We do not supply single pieces or retail quantities.

3. Samples

A pre-production sample is made against your approved size set before bulk cutting. Sample charges, where they apply, are stated in the quotation and are adjusted against the bulk invoice once the order proceeds. Bulk production begins only after you approve the sample in writing.

4. Order confirmation

An order is confirmed when we have your written purchase order and the agreed advance. Capacity is allocated at that point, not before. Lead times quoted run from confirmation and sample approval, whichever is later.

5. Tolerances

Garment manufacturing carries normal, unavoidable variation. Unless your purchase order specifies tighter limits that we have accepted in writing:

  • Measurements may vary by up to 1 cm against the approved sample.
  • Colour may vary slightly between dye lots. Where exact matching matters, ask for a lab dip before bulk.
  • Quantity may vary by up to 3% over or under the ordered amount. Invoicing follows the quantity actually delivered.
  • Fabric weight may vary by up to 5% against the stated GSM.

Variation within these limits is not a defect and is not grounds for rejection.

6. Branding and artwork

You confirm you hold the rights to any artwork, logo, label or trim design you supply, and that using it does not infringe anyone else’s rights. We produce to the artwork as supplied and approved; we are not responsible for errors carried over from approved artwork.

7. Inspection and defects

Please inspect goods on receipt. Report any shortage, damage or manufacturing defect within 7 days of delivery, with photographs and the affected quantity. Confirmed manufacturing defects are replaced or credited at our option. Claims raised after goods have been washed, altered, relabelled or sold on cannot be assessed and will not be accepted.

8. Cancellation

An order may be cancelled before fabric is cut, against the costs already incurred. Once cutting has started the order cannot be cancelled, because cut panels have no other use. Changes requested after confirmation may affect both price and lead time.

9. Payment

Payment terms are stated on the quotation and invoice. Unless agreed otherwise, an advance is payable on confirmation and the balance before dispatch. All invoices are raised under GST 07AIWPG6817F1ZX. Title to the goods passes to you only once payment has been received in full.

10. Delivery

We dispatch pan-India by road transport. Unless agreed otherwise, goods travel at your risk once handed to the transporter, and freight is to your account. Delivery dates are estimates given in good faith; we are not liable for delays caused by transport, strikes, fabric mill delays or other events outside our control.

11. Limitation of liability

Our liability in respect of any order is limited to the invoice value of the goods concerned. We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost profit or lost onward sales.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. The courts at Delhi have jurisdiction.
